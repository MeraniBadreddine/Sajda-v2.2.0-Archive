# Sajda Privacy Policy

Sajda is a local Qibla compass for Android.

## Data we process
- Approximate or precise location, **only on your device**, to compute the direction to the Kaaba.
- Last location (and optional city choice) stored in private app storage (SharedPreferences).

## Data we do not process
- No internet permission: nothing is uploaded.
- No analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs for tracking.

## Control
- You can deny location permission and use a manual city instead.
- In **About**, you can **clear stored location** at any time.
- Uninstalling the app removes local data.

## Contact
This is an independent open-source project. See the repository for issues and updates.
